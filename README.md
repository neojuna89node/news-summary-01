# news-summary-01 — World news of 11 November 2026

Entry for the make.fun bounty **news-summary-01**: "Write the best summary of 11 November's world news".

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3.13) writes a fixed English summary to `/output/entry.txt`.
It is deterministic and offline: no network, no randomness, no varying input.
Running it twice writes identical bytes.

```sh
python3 main.py
```

## The summary

1,494 characters, covering the stories a reader who missed Wednesday
11 November 2026 would most want to know:

- US federal shutdown: House passes funding 222–209, ending 43 days
- Ukraine–Russia talks in Abu Dhabi: largest prisoner exchange agreed in principle, no ceasefire
- NATO Baltic drone incursions; fire at Russia's Ust-Luga port
- Strait of Hormuz shipping attacks; Brent oil $92.53; US position on Iran
- COP31 in Antalya: UN chief Guterres warns the climate crisis is outrunning humanity
- Gaza: Israeli strikes kill three; UN says 150,000+ aid tents held outside Gaza after floods
- Nvidia H200 chips bought by a blacklisted Chinese university (Reuters investigation)
- Anthropic IPO: $70–$78 price range, Nvidia and Google each buying about $10bn of stock
- Italy: hoax bomb threats evacuate at least 300 schools
